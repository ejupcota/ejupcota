About

I am a retired university professor and independent researcher in Biological Sciences, with a long-standing interest in entomology. My scientific interests include insect taxonomy, ecology, agricultural entomology, biodiversity, invasive species, and sustainable pest management.

Research Interests

  • Insect Ecology and Behaviour
  • Agricultural Entomology
